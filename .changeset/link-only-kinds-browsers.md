Link-only sites have kinds. Octo's Shared Browser and the desktop app's Personal Browser refuse a `no-agents` site (its terms bar AI agents or automated access) and every translator's, cache's or archive's copy of one exactly as before, and no longer refuse a `no-reuse` site, whose terms bar copying its content but not reading it. The web-research skill's list records, for every site, the terms page, the day it was checked and the clause it rests on: every listed site stays `no-agents` (a site becomes `no-reuse` only on a quoted clause that bars copying and not reading, and none has one today; RunRepeat publishes no terms of use), and Agoda, Tripadvisor, the Expedia group and Trip.com are marked as barring deep links. The skill says how to use a `no-reuse` site's content (own words, "seen <date> on <host>", a link back, never its reviews, photos or price lists, nothing of it in anything published or shared by public link) and adds a guided mode: for a task that needs a `no-agents` site, Octo gives short numbered steps with a link filled by `instafy web link`, asks the person to open it in their own tab and press Send page to Octo, and works only from the pages they send. Spaces with the earlier, unedited bundled copies of the skill and the list get the new ones. No package changes.
