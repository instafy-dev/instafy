Starter pages read well on a phone. On a phone a comparison's row shows the values found, each with its source line, and names the rest once ("Not found yet: Weight, Price, Two independent reviews", with why when a note says), instead of a label and "Unknown" for every column; a value that can still be checked or opened, or that a page read does not list, stays on its row. The wide table, the row ids and order, Details and "Still to confirm" are as they were. A row's facts take one column when two would cut a value or its source over lines (`site.js` 1.8.0). A store link in a narrow price cell wraps inside it, no caption has an empty line before a part that starts its own line, and on a touch screen each option of a segmented control is at least 44 px wide. A page made before keeps its own copies of `theme.css`, `site.js` and `app.js`.
