A turn that fails and left the person nothing now costs nothing: no reply or note, no step that worked and no file written. Its reserve and daily prompt slot come back (refund reason `failed_turn_without_result`) and its tokens are not charged, also when its machine was lost after a runtime held it. A local drive charged 7 credits for a browser turn whose every page failed to open. A failed turn that left something (a command that worked, a note, a file, a page it opened) is still charged, because a model can be asked to end its own turn without an answer after doing the work. The browser turn that carries on Octo's card, whether Studio opened it or the person pressed the card, is now Instafy's turn: it costs nothing unless it did something in the browser beyond looking at the page it was given (refund reason `browser_card_without_page`), so a turn that reads the page it was put on and stops is free; a reply alone is not charged, since a refusal is a reply. Dispatch stamps such a turn only when it asks the card's own task and nothing else, once per card, for a card from an ordinary turn its sender sent; anything else is the sender's own turn, and a steer makes it theirs. A hand-back or an automatic retry that ends without a reply still costs nothing only when it left the person nothing else either: one that did something (a command that worked, a note, a file) and then failed, was stopped or expired is charged as any turn, so a free resend of a failed turn that left nothing cannot do the work and be stopped for free. A send that carries words for one agent (`agentPromptSegments` or `agentSelection.promptSegments`, which take the place of its own words in that agent's job) is no longer stamped as an automatic retry, and a hand-back that carries some is refused: before, such a send could carry new words on a turn that was free when stopped. `docs/Credits-Billing.md` now says that a retry inside a turn is billed once, from the first attempt, and why a short follow-up often pays for its whole chat's history uncached: the start of each request changes with the turn's lane (building, a question or command, the browser), and one Codex thread serves all three. No package changes.
