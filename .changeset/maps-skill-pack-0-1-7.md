The pinned `interactive-map` skill pack is now `instafy-dev/maps-skill-pack` v0.1.7, with the map fixes from the 28 Oct drive (v0.1.6) and their review fixes (v0.1.7). "3 days in Lisbon … what to see each day" follows the pack's trip guide: a plan with days grouped by area and places to stay, and `uw validate` fails while a stop asked for with nights has no plan. With no stay found, the area to stay in is pinned as a labelled recommendation, never as a hotel. Place cards and the Places list say "Neighbourhood" or "Area" instead of OpenStreetMap's classes, and map data reads "Map data © OpenStreetMap" instead of the search that found it. `uw add-point` and `uw set` take `--props-file` (`-` reads stdin), so a name with an apostrophe no longer breaks a command. The pack's reply rules leave out lookup services, commands and exit codes, and so do the lines its commands give Octo to pass on ("from OpenStreetMap and public registers", not "OSM"). No example puts words in single-quoted props any more. No package changes.
