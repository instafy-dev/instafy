The Browser view shows the page Octo is on while Octo drives, and never a page Octo isn't on. The origin serves a view-only RFB lane (`/browser/vnc/view`, `browser.view`, advertised as `rfb.viewOnly: true`, proxied by the controller) that parses the viewer's half of the protocol and drops every key, pointer, clipboard, resize and power message, so a person who does not drive watches the browser live; it follows Octo into its own tabs, which Octo brings to the front. Studio connects to it with noVNC's `viewOnly` while Octo or someone else holds control, scales the picture instead of resizing the shared screen, and moves back to the driver's lane when control returns. Where a runtime has no such lane, a still picture is kept only for the chat, page and address it was taken on; otherwise the stage says in one line where Octo is ("Octo is on example.com") instead of going black. The view shows one Take over (the reading row's, when it shows), drops the unexplained Keep tab button from the toolbar (the keep route stays), and captions only Octo's steps of the current hold, so no caption outlives the turn. No package changes.
