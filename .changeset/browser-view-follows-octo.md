The Browser view shows the page Octo is on while Octo drives, and never a page Octo isn't on. The origin serves a view-only RFB lane (`/browser/vnc/view`, `browser.view`, advertised as `rfb.viewOnly: true`, proxied by the controller) that parses the viewer's half of the protocol and drops every key, pointer, clipboard, resize and power message, so a person who does not drive watches the browser live; it follows Octo into its own tabs, which Octo brings to the front. Studio connects to it with noVNC's `viewOnly` while Octo or someone else holds control, scales the picture instead of resizing the shared screen, and moves back to the driver's lane when control returns. Where a runtime has no such lane, a still picture is kept only for the chat, page and address it was taken on; otherwise the stage says in one line where Octo is ("Octo is on example.com") instead of going black. The view shows one Take over (the reading row's, when it shows); Take over on one of Octo's own tabs keeps that tab open, in place of the unexplained Keep tab button the toolbar no longer shows. The view captions only Octo's steps of the current hold, even when it opens or first reads the action log during the hold, so no caption from an earlier turn shows and none outlives the turn. No package changes.
