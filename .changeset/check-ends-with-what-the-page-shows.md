`instafy bind check` on a comparison now ends with one line for the reply: how many of its values the page shows, in all and per column, in the page's order ("The page shows 1 of 9 values: Location 1 of 3, What reviewers say 0 of 3, Price for 12–14 February 0 of 3."), and with `--json` the same line as `for_the_reply`, its last key. It counts what the page draws: a value with its page, an estimate, or a price a check read; a value with no page, a page read without the value and a dash count as none. On a page from what Octo knows the line says none is checked. The card's command, or the step its card waits for, is still named as the next step, just before that line. A reply had said a comparison page covered location, reviews and prices for three hotels when the page showed one of those nine values.
