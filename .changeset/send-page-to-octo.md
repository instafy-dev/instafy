Studio's Personal and Shared Browser toolbars have **Send page to Octo**. A press reads the page on screen once, masks passwords and keys where the page is (in Desktop, or on the workspace browser's own machine), and stages a Markdown copy in the composer that records the page's address, title, read time and browser, with the page's text marked as untrusted page content. The person sends it with their next message, which goes to Octo in chat. A page that seems to show a secret is not attached, and the press gives Octo no access to either browser. No package changes.
