`instafy preview open` gives the preview card a picture and a facts line. Once the site is live it loads the page in the runtime's own headless Chromium and uploads a 640×400 JPEG of it, with the page's own `<meta name="description">`, within 15 s; without a browser it uses the page's `og:image` when that is on the site itself. `--summary "<text>"` sets the card's facts line (1 to 80 characters), `--thumbnail <file>` uploads a picture a skill made itself (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 512 KiB and 2000×2000) and `--no-thumbnail` uploads none. The JSON gains `thumbnail` (`uploaded`, `skipped` or `failed`) and `thumbnailReason`; the exit code never changes. `instafy preview check` refreshes the picture of a preview open on its port and path. Both load only the site's own origin directly: other public addresses go through the runtime image's egress proxy, and another local port, private and reserved addresses and link-only sites are blocked (listed under `blocked` in `check`'s report, not as page errors). Without the egress proxy nothing off the site's origin loads.
