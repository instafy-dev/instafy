Add `instafy web settings` to configure a space's research allowance and `instafy web budget` to inspect remaining reads. Public page reads share controller-enforced task and per-site limits across the parent, helpers and continued worker jobs. Default to 100 fresh page loads per task and 25 per site, including standalone use. Cached reads remain free, and saved settings and running tasks keep their explicit limits.
