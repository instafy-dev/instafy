The compare starter writes the line under a comparison's title itself, from the page's own sorts and the cells of the rows it shows ("Lowest starting price and lowest weight: Laptop 12"): a sort names the row it puts first when every row shows a value for it, none of them one that may have changed, and they are not all the same; with nothing decided there is no line, and a page from what Octo knows says "From what Octo knows; nothing checked." there. A `lede` in a comparison's `data.json` is no longer drawn, so such a page still loads. `instafy bind check` no longer stops at an item whose words a page's script holds where it can't read them (text drawn with `document.createElement`, or HTML from a string): in the same step it posts a new page's card, as a passing check does, has `instafy preview check` load the page where it is served, on both sizes, and answers with what the page drew, failing for an item the page doesn't draw or an error on it, and saying it can't tell only when nothing could look at it. It also names each line of the page's own files that sets HTML from a string (`innerHTML`, `outerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`), to draw with `site.h` and `site.trust(fact)` instead; that never fails the page. Pages made before keep their own copies of `app.js` and `site.js`.
