A turn's activity line holds still and says what the turn made. While a turn runs, each label stays at least 1.2 seconds and a label replaced before its turn is never shown, so a step that ends at once no longer flashes past; "Thinking…" shows only after 3 seconds with no step, and "Waiting for you" shows at once. A turn opens with one "Starting…" instead of "Octo is starting its workspace…", "Octo is waiting for its turn…" and "Thinking…" inside a second; the typing row before the turn's line follows the same rule. Once done, what the turn made leads the line ("Built the map, looked up 14 places, read 6 pages"), and a turn that made nothing labelled but opened a preview leads with "Opened a preview". A step that failed never counts as done: when nothing else is left, the line says what was tried ("Tried searching the web"). The runtime names `instafy preview close` "Stopped the preview" once it exits 0. In Details a step is named by its first command that is not quiet, so `uw geocode Baixa; uw stay …` reads "Found places to stay"; a finished row reads in the past tense ("Opened a preview", "Checked the page"), a failed one as what it tried ("Tried using the browser"), and a row with no label reads "Checked the space", with the command only as its second line. No package changes.
