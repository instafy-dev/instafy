`instafy web read`, in an Instafy turn, no longer reads an address past a site's home page that no message, page read or search result gave the turn. It answers `skipped` with `code: "not_given"`, fetches nothing for it (not even robots.txt), remembers no stop, and names the site's home page to read instead, never "Give the person the plain link."; a search address on a site nobody gave names no home page. The turn's record is the folder the runtime names in `INSTAFY_TURN_GIVEN_DIR`, kept the way Instafy's browser keeps its own: fingerprints of each address's site and path, never an address. Each page the reader reads adds its own address, its links and the addresses its text spells out, and `instafy web search` adds its results. Without that folder, as on a person's own computer, nothing is refused, and nor is anything after one of the turn's searches lists none of the pages it found (its record is then marked `anyAddress`). A stop the turn or the workspace already holds for the address or its site answers first, with its own words, and an address a text spells out ends where its sentence's own marks begin, those of Chinese, Japanese and Korean too (`。`, `、`, `（`), so the address a person's message gave in such a sentence is read. A reader asked for a product address no page gave read it, and the turn's reply and card passed it on as the shop's own page. A read without `--find` now returns up to 12,000 characters (it was 4,000; `--find` passages keep 4,000), and a cut read says so on its text line, not only in its JSON: a status page cut at 4,000 characters came back without its end.
