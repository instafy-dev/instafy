`@instafy/browser-action-policy` has `formSubmission.js`, the one place both browsers compute the submission a form step makes, as the browser does: a submit button's `formaction`, `formmethod`, `formenctype` and `formtarget` over the form's own, Enter through the form's default button (the first submit button in tree order, one joined with `form=` included, however many controls the page has; a disabled one blocks it), a click followed up its event path through slots and shadow roots to the submit button it reaches (labels, plain buttons, text areas and fields pass it on; links, checkboxes and summaries take it), `<base href>` (an action of only spaces is the page itself) and `<base target>` (also for an empty target), frame names, and `method="dialog"`, which sends nothing. A field inside a submit button, which a click and Enter send in different ways, is reported as `nested`, and `stepSubmission` marks its steps for the hosts to refuse. The step classifier judges those facts: a same-site GET search is free only when its submission is a known GET of the page's own site in the same tab, to an address that is not risky, and a submission to a risky address (sign-out, export) takes that address's category. `formSubmissionCases.json` is one table of form steps both browsers' tests run. No package changes.
