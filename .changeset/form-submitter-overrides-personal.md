In the desktop app's Personal Browser, Octo's form steps are judged and taken by the submission the browser makes: the default button's `formaction`, `formmethod` and `formtarget` for Enter, a submit button's own for a click, `<base href>` for relative actions. A form sent to another site is held to the link-only and private-address rules and, in Ask mode, asks for that site in the tab before the step's dialog, which now says where the form goes. Enter in a search field whose default button posts to another site now asks, and then sends what the browser would send. A form step that would open another window is refused with `new_window_blocked`, and one through a field inside a submit button with `form_submission_unclear`. A page's keydown handler that moves where Enter sends the form stops the step before anything is sent. Enter now clicks the default button the way the browser does (a disabled one blocks it) instead of submitting the form without its button, and typing that changes where a form goes is checked again before Enter. No package changes.
