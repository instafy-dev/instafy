`instafy bind check` now fails a page that loads a file from outside its folder: `../another-page/site.js` in an element, a style or a script (also through a `<base href>`), a path from the root that names no file of its folder but another folder's (`/sites/another-page/site.js`), or a link in its folder to another folder's file. A page is served from its own folder, so there it couldn't load it, and it would change whenever that other folder does. The check names the `instafy bind init <dir> --starter <name>` that gives the page its own files (the starter whose script it borrowed, else `blank`). In a space, while no preview shows the folder, a check that passes on a new page (one the space's record of its pages doesn't hold yet) now serves it and posts its card itself, titled from the page (`data.json`'s title, else the page's own), and prints what `instafy preview open` answered; any other check still ends with the card's command, except for a page that loads files from outside its folder. A page the record holds already had its card (in this chat or another, and maybe stopped since), so its check only names the command. On a phone, the compare starter names every missing value once, under "Still missing", with its note, in place of "Not found yet" and "Not checked". Pages made before keep their own copies of `app.js`.
