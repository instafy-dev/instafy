No more turns nobody asked for after a browser turn, and no JSON under a browser reply. Octo's `request_browser` card now says whether Octo builds something from what the browser finds (`continueBuilding`, a page or a map); only such a card hands the browser's findings back, and only when its browser turn read at least one page. A question ("find the opening hours", "how do I get there by tram") is answered by the browser turn itself, so it is no longer followed by a second turn that repeats the answer or opens another browser card. A hand-back turn can no longer ask for the browser: the runtime drops its card and leaves the browser rule out of its prompt, and it is told not to repeat the browser turn's answer. Only a browser turn that will hand back is asked to end its reply with finding lines, and the runtime moves any such lines out of the reply the person reads into one note in the turn's Details, where the controller reads them back for the hand-back. The controller refuses a hand-back for a question's card (`browser_hand_back_not_building`) or for a browser turn that read no page (`browser_hand_back_nothing_read`). No package changes.
