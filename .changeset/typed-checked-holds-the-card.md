A page that says "checked <date>" or "seen <date>" in typed words now waits for its card until those words are deleted. `instafy bind check` says, for each such line, the words to delete, and no longer quotes what `site.trust` draws as its example; a second check that finds typed words says that rewording never passes. While they are there the check names no card command, `instafy bind card` posts none and answers with the check's lines (`typed` in `--json`), and `instafy preview open` for a page `instafy bind serve` serves answers with the same lines (exit 1) and starts nothing, so a check and a card command joined by `;`, and a page written by hand that no check ran on, meet the same scan. Every other finding of a failing check still ends with the card's command. A page went out saying a journey planner nobody ran was "checked 20 Nov 2026" after its check had named that line twice.
