Three smaller sign-in leftovers on the phone apps and Desktop are gone. A GitHub or Google sign-in that Android finishes in a new process (it ended the app while the sign-in sheet was open) lands where it started, such as the invite or space you were opening, instead of always on Studio: the destination is kept beside the started attempt in localStorage for up to 30 minutes (web sign-ins keep it in their tab only), and while that sign-in is pending it wins over the `?redirect=/studio` that the app's start page bounces to; a completed sign-in forgets it. A reload of the app's page (a live update, a controller-requested reload, iOS restarting its web view) no longer hands the code that launched the app to a fresh page, which exchanged it again and showed "Sign-in could not be completed on this device" after a good sign-in; claimed codes are remembered, as hashes, for the life of the process. And a sign-in error the login page already showed is no longer shown a second time the next time the login page opens. No package changes.
