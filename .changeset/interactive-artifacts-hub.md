Every space now gets two generic skills, kept current by the runtime and the controller's managed defaults. `instafy-interactive-artifacts` is the hub for answers that should be a page people tap through (a map, ranking, comparison or shopping table, dashboard, quiz, timeline, planner, tracker or checklist): it picks the builder from the skills installed in the space (a skill pack's own skill when one covers that kind of page, `instafy-bound-sites` for values checked per click, otherwise a starter site), builds with sources and dates, serves the site and shows it with `instafy preview open`, then replies in 2-5 lines with 2-3 suggested replies. `instafy-research-team` splits research of many items (a long trip, several products) into parallel `multi_agent_plan` lanes with one handoff file each (rules in `references/researcher.md`), then builds one page from them at the final checkpoint. The skill router sends pages to tap through to the hub, treats an unavailable outside lookup as unavailable rather than missing, and `instafy-location-sharing` now routes only on the user's own position ("near me"), not on a place they named. No package changes.
