In the desktop app's Personal Browser, a page Octo opened stays Octo's when you take control back: it keeps running, but if it, or a page it opens in turn, reaches a private or local address meanwhile, Octo does not read or use it after you resume. Pages Octo opened also send no request, WebSockets included, to a private address literal or local name, and a WebSocket's name is checked before it connects. A form Octo submits to a link-only site or a private address is refused before anything asks, like a link. Octo's status no longer names a link-only site you have open in a tab it does not drive, or while it is paused, and a page load stopped after its previous step is reported as that step's, not as a refusal of the next one. No package changes.
