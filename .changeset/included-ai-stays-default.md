Instafy AI stays the AI a person's spaces use when they connect their own AI, unless they choose that connection: connecting from chat ("Connect your own AI", the AI gate) chooses it, and so does "Make default" in the Your AI panel. Connecting from the Your AI panel or an agent profile no longer takes the default from an available Instafy AI, and the controller makes a new connection the default by itself only when Instafy AI cannot run the person's turns. Defaults saved earlier are kept. The Your AI panel no longer calls Instafy AI "Currently unavailable" because the person's own AI is the default: `managedAi.available` says whether Instafy AI can run a turn, and `managedAi.unavailableReason` says why not (`disabled`, `no_proxy`, `proxy_unreachable`, `proxy_needs_credential` or `daily_limit_reached`), which the panel puts in plain words. A hosted Studio tab outside Instafy Desktop no longer asks the extension host on this computer, which only answers Desktop and local development pages, so the Extensions panel shows "No extensions yet" instead of "Local providers unavailable — Failed to fetch". No package changes.
