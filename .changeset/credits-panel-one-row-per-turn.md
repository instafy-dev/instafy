The Credits panel's activity log shows one row per Instafy AI turn, named by the chat it was sent in, with what the turn cost in all: "Plan 3 days in Lisbon · −14 credits", where it showed two rows at different times, "AI usage −1" and "AI adjustment −13", each reading "Instafy AI · GPT-6 Luna · OpenAI · 66k tokens". A turn that cost nothing in the end has no row, and no row shows the model, the AI vendor, a token count or an id; the ledger keeps them. Amounts of one read "1 credit". `GET /credits/ledger` adds `chatName` to a turn's rows when the viewer may open that chat, by Home's rule; a teammate's private chat, or a chat in a space the viewer is not in, stays "A chat". The line under the balance reads "Tops back up to 800 credits once a day": the ledger tops a balance up the first time it finds it under the limit that UTC day, which can be at any hour, so the line no longer says "at 00:00 UTC", and `docs/Credits-Billing.md` now says what the ledger does. No migration, and no package changes.
