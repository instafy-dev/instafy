The pinned `interactive-map` skill pack is now `instafy-dev/maps-skill-pack` v0.1.11. A place on a map shows its kind only as a plain word ("Museum", "Square") or not at all, never as a map tag such as "Board". A square that shares its name with the station next to it is put on the map as the square, under the name asked for, not as the station. Finding places to stay keeps a hotel among the picks when one is in range, and says so when none is, rather than listing only hostels. On a phone, a raised map panel shows its first places whole, its list ends just above the chat's composer instead of running on under it, and tapping its search box no longer moves the panel. The map's credits name OpenStreetMap once. No package changes.
