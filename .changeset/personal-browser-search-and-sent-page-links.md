The desktop app's Personal Browser searches from its address bar: words that are not an address ("best ramen tokyo", "ramen") open a search on the engine the person picks under **Search with** in the browser's settings, while addresses such as `example.com`, `localhost:3000` and `192.168.1.1` open as before. The engines and their search links are the web-research skill's data (`references/search-engines.json`), bundled into Desktop, which checks them and builds each search's address; Octo never searches through them, and the Shared Browser does not search. **Send page to Octo** now also sends up to 200 of the page's web links, each its text and address, masked like the page: `javascript:` and `data:` links are dropped, a link to a link-only site is marked with the site's name, and the links arrive in their own untrusted block after the page's text. A browser that cannot load the link-only list sends no links. No package changes.
