Long turns now cost their real tokens, so they cost more than before. Until now a turn whose run log was long, as most maps, pages and browser tasks are, reached the controller without its token count and was charged only its 1-credit reserve, while a short turn ("stop the preview server") paid for its tokens. The runtime kept only the first 80 entries of every list in a completion, and the token count is in the log's last entry. Now every Instafy AI turn is charged for its own tokens: a turn that cost 1 credit before, such as building a map or a comparison page, can cost about 5 to 20 credits at the default rates. A turn Instafy starts by itself, a browser hand-back or an automatic retry, costs nothing unless it ends with Octo's reply: one that fails, is stopped, expires, ends without a reply or has its team plan refused gets its reserve and daily prompt slot back (refund reason `system_turn_without_reply`), and its tokens are not charged. A posted `NO_RESPONSE` counts as a reply; only an evaluation turn's decline, which posts nothing, does not. Billing trusts only the controller's own marks: dispatch marks a resend as Instafy's own automatic retry only when it repeats the failed prompt word for word, with no attachments and no browser, a hand-back always asks Studio's own words and carries no attachments, and words someone sends into either kind while it runs make it a turn they sent. A browser turn Studio opens by itself for Octo's card carries on the person's own request and is charged as theirs. No package changes.
