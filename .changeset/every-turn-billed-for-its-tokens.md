Every Instafy AI turn is charged for its own tokens, and a turn Instafy starts by itself costs nothing unless it ends with Octo's reply. The runtime cut every list in a completion to its first 80 entries, so a turn whose run log had more than 80 events (a map, a page, a browser task: most real turns) reached the controller without the closing `turn.completed` that carries its usage and cost only its 1-credit reserve, while a short turn ("stop the preview server") was charged its tokens. A long run log now keeps its first and last events within that limit, so the usage always arrives. A browser hand-back or an automatic retry that fails, is stopped, expires or ends without a reply gets its reserve and daily prompt slot back (refund reason `system_turn_without_reply`), and its tokens are not charged. Billing reads only the controller's own marks: dispatch marks a resend as Instafy's own (`automaticRetry`) only after checking the failure it retries, and drops any client copy. No package changes.
