Studio's chats keep their names. An untitled chat is **New chat** everywhere (the chat list, its tab, Home and every device), never "Conversation 3" counted from its place in the list, so a chat no longer becomes "Conversation 1" once opened while the others are renumbered. As soon as its first prompt is sent, the sender's client names the chat from that prompt's first line, cut to about 40 characters at a word with "…" ("Plan 3 days in Lisbon and make a map…"), and stores the name on the chat, so it has one while its first turn runs and after that turn fails. Small numbers (a count up to 99, or a year) now stay in the name; a longer number still ends it, and a message about a password, PIN, login, secret, token or key, or holding something that looks like one, stays **New chat**. A model title, when the person has their own AI credential, replaces that name once unless someone renamed the chat meanwhile. Home's activity rows use the chat's real name, the one this device shows or else the stored one, instead of a bare "Conversation"; a run that belongs to no chat is named after its space, not "New chat". Older chats stored as "Conversation 3" keep that name until they are titled. No package changes.
