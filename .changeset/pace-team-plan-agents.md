Team plan agents (lanes, follow-on agents, their continuations and retries) now start in step with their AI upstream across all spaces, instead of failing on its rate limit. The upstream is the agent's own credential, or the platform's model proxy. When any run on an upstream stops on a short provider rate limit, the controller records the stated wait and lowers how many plan agents of that upstream may run at once to half of those running then. Until the wait has passed and while the upstream is at that ceiling, its plan agents stay queued: the group stays open and the lead is not woken. Each plan agent that ends without a rate limit raises the ceiling a little, at most half of it may start within ten seconds, and what is learned is forgotten an hour after the last rate limit. `INSTAFY_UPSTREAM_PLAN_WORKER_CEILING` sets a fixed ceiling for a small upstream from the first burst on. Leads and ordinary turns are never held. New table `upstream_pacing`, controller only. No package changes.
