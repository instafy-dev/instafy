A team plan can now declare how long its team has (`timeBudgetMinutes`, nullable in the strict plan schema), and the team answers within it. The runtime passes the declared budget on to the controller, which starts one clock when it accepts the plan and carries it in `multiAgentPlan.teamClock` on every job of the team: its lanes, their continuations and rate-limit retries, its lead checkpoints, and agents a lead starts again. A plan that declares none has no clock; an operator can cap every team with `INSTAFY_TEAM_TIME_BUDGET_SECONDS`, or turn clocks off with 0. The runtime ends each turn of the team by the deadline, counting from the time left the controller states at lease, wraps it up within its last quarter when the deadline cut it short, and offers a note for a next turn only when one still fits with a minute to spare. No plan agent starts once less than 3 minutes is left, however it was queued: the lease passes it over and the idle sweep settles it as not started ("the team's time budget ran out"), so the lead's checkpoint comes with that outcome. A lane whose turn ran out of time and got no further turn is shown to the lead as unfinished, with its note on what is left, and a lead checkpoint that comes late is told to answer with what is ready, list what is unfinished or not started, and start no more agents. No package changes.
