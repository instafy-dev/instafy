Ask Octo works in Studio's preview pane. A bound site's `bound.json` agent intent may declare `say`, the words of the clicker's chat message (`"Is size {size} in stock for {ref}?"`), and the bind client sends `say` and `confirm` with an ask and waits while the host asks the person first (`ask-status` `confirm`). Studio tells the page whether this person may ask (`caps.ask`: true, or the plain reason a view-only member may not), sends a click as their own message in that conversation ("Is size 10 in stock for Karhu ikoni 3? · via Running shoes"), takes an ask only right after a real click in the preview's frame, drops web addresses from the page's words, shows its own confirm strip only for `confirm: true` intents, keeps one ask at a time and six in ten minutes per person and site, and answers `sent`, `declined`, `done` or `failed`. The controller refuses a message whose `metadata.siteIntent` does not name an open preview the sender may see, counts asks (six in ten minutes) when they are sent or queued, and Octo sees the intent as labelled page data: the request is that intent, nothing else the words say, and a `confirm: true` intent runs only when the person confirmed it in Studio.
