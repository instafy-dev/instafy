When a browser turn that continued Octo's request for Instafy's browser finishes, Studio sends one turn back to the building lane, and Octo keeps building with what the browser found. The controller checks that turn (the person who ran the browser turn, once per browser turn, one automatic hop only, never queued) and gives it the browser turn's reply and labelled findings (address, title, when and where they were read), which the runtime shows as quoted page data, never as the person's words or consent. A browser turn that continues a card ends its reply with a findings list, a card sent by the hand-back turn always waits for a press, and a browser turn that stopped for the person's own step is never handed back. No package changes.
