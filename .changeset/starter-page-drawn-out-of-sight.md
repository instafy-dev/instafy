A starter page tells the Studio preview it has drawn on its next frame, or on a 100 ms timer when no frame comes first. A page out of sight (a hidden tab or pane) gets no frames, so its preview kept saying it was opening the site for the full 15 s; it now says it has drawn within about a second at most, as the browser may hold an unseen page's timers that long (`site.js` 1.6.1). The compare starter leaves out a sort whose column no row has a value for (a price a store can still check stays): a phone no longer shows "Unknown" on the right of every row, and "Sort by" offers only the sorts left. Rows whose `data.json` asks for a sort are still ordered by the first one left, and a `sort` that is not a list orders nothing instead of stopping the page. Its notes on `data.json` say that a value cites only a page that gives it, so a count of what was found cites only what it counts, and that the lede says only what a page you read says. `instafy bind check` asks for the page that gives a value, or, when no page gives it, a note saying why.
