A starter page tells the Studio preview it has drawn on its next frame, or 100 ms after drawing when no frame comes. A page out of sight (a hidden tab or pane) gets no frames, so its preview kept saying it was opening the site for the full 15 s; it now shows the page as soon as it is drawn (`site.js` 1.6.1). The compare starter leaves out a sort whose column no row has a value for (a price a store can still check stays): a phone no longer shows "Unknown" on the right of every row, and "Sort by" offers only sorts that order something. Its notes on `data.json` say that a value cites only a page that gives it, so a count of what was found cites only what it counts, and that the lede says only what a page you read says.
