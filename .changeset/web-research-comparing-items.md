The web-research skill says how to compare items: per item, a price from the maker's or a shop's own page, or "price unknown" with why and another way to check it (a search with the space's key, reference search for shops' official sites, a guided link to a link-only shop, or the page for the person to send to Octo), never a remembered or guessed price; the number of independent reviews the person asked for, each of that exact model, or "1 of 2 reviews found" in the reply and on the page; and never an item without a price as within a stated limit. A search may use an item's name as its official page gives it. The interactive-artifacts hub, the bound-sites skill and its cheat sheet say the same for pages of options. A research-team lane reads with the research skill and compares its product by "Comparing items" (a price or `"check: price (<why>)"`, and the reviews asked for or `"check: 1 of 2 reviews found"`). Spaces with the earlier, unedited copies get the new ones. A fixture eval of a shopping comparison checks each rule. No package changes.
