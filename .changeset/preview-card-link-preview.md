The site preview card in a conversation looks like a link preview: a picture of the site on top, its title with a lock ("Only space members can open this") and a chevron, one line of facts (the skill's `--summary`, else the page's own description), and a status line only when the site is not simply live ("Checking the site…" after 3 s, "Starting…", "Not responding", "Stopped · opening restarts it", "Stopped · only people who can edit this space can start it", "Stopped", "Couldn't start · …"). The whole card is one button named "Open {title}"; the "Ready to preview" overline and the button row are gone, and a card that cannot be pressed no longer looks pressable. The controller keeps one picture per preview in a new controller-only table (`runtime_preview_thumbnails`, RLS on with no client policy), uploaded only by the preview's own runtime, checked by its bytes (JPEG, PNG or WebP), stripped of metadata, and readable only by members who may open the preview (`private, no-store`). Studio fetches it with the person's own session into memory, never a signed URL or a frame. The picture goes with every new open of the preview, a close or a replacement, and `preview.thumbnail_updated` tells the card a new one is there. No package changes.
