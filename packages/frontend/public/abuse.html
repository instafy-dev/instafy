Report abuse

Sites on instafy.site, and on the other addresses that Instafy tunnels and previews use, are made and run by Instafy users, not by Instafy. If one of them is harmful, tell us and we will look into it.

How to report

Email contact@instafy.dev with the subject “Abuse report”. For a security issue in Instafy itself, see our security policy.

What to include

  • The full address of the page (for example, https://name.instafy.site/page).
  • What is wrong with it (for example, phishing, malware, spam, or content that infringes your rights).
  • When you saw it, and a screenshot if you have one.
  • How we can reach you if we have questions.

Please don’t send passwords or other people’s personal data.

What happens next

We read every report and aim to reply within two business days. When a site breaks our Terms of Use, we take it down and may suspend the account that runs it.
